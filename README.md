## Why bother

Three reasons, in the order people usually arrive at them.

**Privacy.** The text never leaves the machine. That matters for anything you
would hesitate to paste into a browser — customer records, contracts, draft
work that is not yours to share.

**No metering.** No per-token cost, no rate limit, no account.

**It works offline.** Turn the wifi off and it carries on.

What you give up is capability. A model small enough to run on a laptop is
not as good as the large hosted ones, and it knows nothing that happened
after it was trained. The useful question is not which is better, it is which
jobs are small enough to hand to the one on your desk.

## Environment

Windows 11 · Ollama · a laptop with 32 GB RAM and an RTX 3050 Ti with 4 GB
of video memory, which turns out to be a more useful machine to demonstrate
on than a workstation.
